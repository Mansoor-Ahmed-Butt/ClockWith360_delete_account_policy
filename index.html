How to delete your account

ClockWith360 is used by organizations to manage their employees' attendance, leave, and HR records. Because each organization controls its own employee accounts, account deletion is handled through a request process rather than an automatic in-app button. This page explains exactly how to request deletion of your account and your data.

Steps to request deletion

1

Contact your organization's Admin

Your employer's designated Admin manages your account within ClockWith360 and can submit a deletion request on your behalf. Send your request to your HR department or whoever manages your organization's ClockWith360 account, asking them to delete your account.

2

No Admin available, or you'd prefer to contact us directly

If you cannot reach your organization's Admin, or your employment has ended and you no longer have a way to contact them, email us directly at the address below. Include your registered email address and your organization's name so we can verify and process the request.

3

Confirmation

Once a deletion request is verified, your account and associated personal data will be deleted within 30 days, except where retention is required by law (see below).

What gets deleted

Data typeOutcome
Profile information (name, email, photo)Deleted
Login credentialsDeleted
Device push notification tokensDeleted
Future location trackingStopped immediately upon deletion

What may be retained, and why

Some data may be retained for a limited period after account deletion, where required for legal, financial, or labor recordkeeping purposes:

Data typeRetention reason
Historical attendance records (check-in/check-out timestamps)Labor law and payroll recordkeeping requirements that may apply to your employer
Historical leave recordsHR and employment dispute resolution purposes
Historical location data tied to past attendance entriesRetained only as long as necessary to support the attendance records above, then deleted or anonymized
Retention periods for the records above are set by your employing organization's own policy and applicable local labor law, and are typically no longer than is legally required. If you have questions about your specific organization's retention period, your Admin or HR department can confirm.

For organizations (Admins)

If you are an Admin managing your organization's ClockWith360 account, you can delete an employee's account directly through the Admin dashboard under Employees → select employee → Remove account. This immediately revokes their login access and stops any further location tracking associated with their device.
